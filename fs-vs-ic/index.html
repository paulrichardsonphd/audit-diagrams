Material weakness

A material weakness is a deficiency, or a combination of deficiencies, in internal control over financial reporting, such that there is a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement of the company’s annual or interim financial statements will not be prevented or detected on a timely basis.In plain terms: a problem in the process. The controls might not prevent or catch a material misstatement, even if the F/S turn out to be right.Source: PCAOB AS 2201.A7 (exact wording)

1 material weakness = adverse opinion on internal control. If there are deficiencies that, individually or in combination, result in one or more material weaknesses, the auditor must express an adverse opinion on the company’s internal control over financial reporting, unless there is a restriction on the scope of the engagement.Source: PCAOB AS 2201.90 (exact wording)

Public companies: the auditor’s opinion on internal control is required only for accelerated and large accelerated filers (SOX 404(b)), and that audit is performed together with the F/S audit (an integrated audit, AS 2201). Smaller public companies include management’s own assessment of internal control, without an auditor’s opinion on it. For private companies both are optional.
