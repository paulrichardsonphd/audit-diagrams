Information risk

Information risk reflects the possibility that the information upon which the business risk decision was made was inaccurate. A likely cause of the information risk is the possibility of inaccurate financial statements.

In plain terms: the risk that the numbers people base decisions on are wrong. Investors and lenders face it because they can’t check the F/S themselves; an audit reduces it. Source: Arens et al., Ch. 1 (exact wording)

Public company · required

shares are public→audits mandatory (SEC)
F/S audit (all) + IC audit (accelerated filers)=integrated audit

Quarterly F/S (the 10-Q) are reviewed, not audited.

Private company · optional

audits when users want them→bank, owners

The F/S audit and the IC audit are both optional.

Reasons to audit → information risk; learn from the audit
